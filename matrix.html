Feature matrix

Every board, every feature: whether the board has it, whether it is on by default, and how far it has actually been run. Pick a board, or open a maker below. The same facts in words are on Test status; what to have at hand before you flash is on Before you flash.

STM32 users: keep an ST-Link (SWD) connected. No STM32 flash write has ever run on a real chip: QEMU has no STM32 flash controller, so the erase and program drivers are written from the reference manuals and have never executed. Uploads, the boot log, confirming an image and the rollback floors all write flash. Until a board has been tried, wire SWD (an ST-Link, or the one on a Nucleo), make sure STM32CubeProgrammer can connect under reset and mass-erase it, and don't touch readout protection (RDP level 2 can't be undone). Holding BOOT0 high at reset starts the chip's own ROM loader, whatever the flash holds. Details for every family.
